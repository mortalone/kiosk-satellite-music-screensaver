# Reference-driven visual direction

Reviewed the supplied current-display MP4, Cotodama MP4 and animated ferrofluid WEBP as decoded frame sequences. Direct YouTube/Instagram pages were not playable in the review environment. Audio alignment was not assessed from those sequences.

## What the previous implementation missed

The previous ferrofluid contour had six permanently connected radial lobes. Satellites followed regular angles around a circular chamber, so it could wobble but not genuinely change topology. A hard bezel and glass wedge framed the effect. Missing audio triggered a synthetic 124 BPM pattern unrelated to the song. These choices explain the repetitive appearance.

The previous lyric view was a ScrollView with highlighted rows, not the reference's changing typographic compositions.

## Direction for further work (including Antigravity)

Ferrofluid should read as a heavy, glossy black substance inside a softly lit cavity. Motion should have inertia, deformation, irregular extension, detachment and reunion. Keep most of the mass around the central stage. Avoid evenly spaced petals, orbiting dots, repeating sine patterns, visible bezels and hard rectangular containers. Silence should settle rather than manufacture kicks. The new implicit surface permits separation, but is an approximation; it is not a magnetic fluid simulation. Tune against video, not an isolated attractive frame.

Lyrics should make the current phrase the composition: alternate centered serif, asymmetric stacked words, diagonal blocks and contrasting scales. Preserve every word and legibility. Do not show a long scrolling list. Line-synced LRC cannot provide exact word timing; staggered entrances are animation only. Without timecodes the current implementation displays a static excerpt with an explicit label, not simulated synchronization.

## Device acceptance pass

1. Record 30 seconds each of quiet, percussive and sustained music with live audio frames. Compare the same segments with the old version.
2. Confirm deformation changes with audio, detached masses rejoin, and no obvious cyclic orbit or fixed petal count appears.
3. Disconnect the audio feed: fake tempo must not continue; the missing-feed label should appear.
4. Pause, resume and seek across lyric timestamps. Check all four compositions and long phrases in portrait, landscape and split mode, with no clipped words or playback-control overlap.
5. Measure frame times/thermals on the actual Pi/tablet, including Eco mode. The cached raster limits allocation but does not establish a performance guarantee.

This first revision needs that on-device pass before claiming visual parity with the references. More elaborate text choreography and a finer fluid field can follow based on recordings.
